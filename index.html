Events shouldn’t live in ten group chats.

Today students chase posters, WhatsApp groups and separate forms. Societies juggle spreadsheets. Eventra gives each group one place to work.

Students

Find it, book it, show up.

  • Browse and filter every event by category, society or date
  • Register free or buy tickets in one flow
  • Keep tickets in a personal digital wallet
  • Get reminders and picks based on your interests

Societies

Run events without the spreadsheet.

  • A dashboard for your society and committee
  • Publish events with posters and ticket categories
  • Track registrations, sales and attendance live
  • Request venues and equipment, with clash warnings

Administrators

See what’s happening on campus.

  • Verify societies and approve events
  • Monitor university-wide activity in one view
  • Manage venues, resources and permissions
  • Spot scheduling conflicts before they happen

From idea to full house in four steps.

  1. Society publishes

    Create the event, set ticket types and capacity, and request a venue.

  2. Admin approves

    The event is reviewed and goes live for students to discover.

  3. Student books

    Register or pay online and get a unique QR ticket instantly.

  4. Staff scan at the door

    Tickets are checked in seconds and attendance updates live.

Privacy notice

When you pre-register, we collect your name, university email address and the role you select. We may also see basic, anonymous visit statistics.

  • Why: only to contact you about Eventra early access and launch. We do not sell your data or use it for unrelated marketing.
  • Who sees it: the Eventra project team, and the hosting providers that store it for us.
  • How long: until early access has launched and our project evaluation is complete, or sooner if you ask us to delete it.
  • Your rights: you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent, by emailing .

We aim to handle personal data in line with Sri Lanka’s Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022.
